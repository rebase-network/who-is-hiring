Role Overview

EkvahLabs is looking for an experienced **Blockchain Developer** to join our engineering team and help build and maintain blockchain-based applications, Web3 products, and infrastructure. The role focuses on developing decentralized features, blockchain integrations, smart contracts, wallet systems, transaction flows, and supporting backend infrastructure. You’ll work with technologies such as: - **Solidity** - **TypeScript** - **Node.js** - **Ethers.js / Web3 libraries** - **Smart Contracts** - Blockchain APIs and infrastructure - Wallet integrations - NFT and Web3 technologies

Required Skills & Experience

- Strong professional experience in **blockchain / Web3 development** - Strong knowledge of **Solidity and smart contract development** - Strong **TypeScript / Node.js** experience - Experience with **Ethers.js, Web3.js, or similar Web3 libraries** - Experience with blockchain wallets and transaction systems - Understanding of EVM-based blockchain networks - Experience integrating blockchain systems with APIs and backend services - Strong understanding of blockchain security and common smart contract vulnerabilities - Experience with Git and modern software development workflows - Strong debugging and problem-solving skills

Nice to Have

The following experience would be highly valuable: - **Full-stack development experience, preferably 7+ years** - Professional experience working with **cryptocurrency exchanges** - Experience building or maintaining exchange-related systems, trading infrastructure, wallets, deposits/withdrawals, or transaction processing - **Deep understanding of NFTs**, including NFT standards, marketplaces, minting, metadata, ownership, transfers, and smart contract architecture - Experience with NFT marketplaces or NFT-based applications - Experience with DeFi protocols and decentralized applications - Experience with cryptocurrency payment systems - Experience with blockchain gaming or Web3 gaming platforms - Experience working with multiple EVM-compatible networks - Experience with cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, and production deployments

Location & Timezone

**Location:** Ontario, Canada We are open to candidates working remotely from different locations. **Timezone:** Flexible. Candidates do not need to be located in the same timezone, although reasonable availability for collaboration with the engineering team is expected.

Compensation

**9,000–12,000/month**, depending on experience, technical expertise, and overall fit. There is flexibility for exceptional candidates with significant blockchain, exchange, NFT, or full-stack experience.
